NEON HARBOR

An open-world crime story · Port Delmar

You are Rook Calloway, a courier driver with a fast car, a faster mouth, and a debt to the wrong people. The fixer Marla Voss has work for you — deliveries, getaways, and one last job on the docks. Take the work, take the cars, take the city. Just don't get caught.

HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD to move, mouse to look, click to shoot your pistol, Q to punch. Walk up to any parked or slow car and press E to steal it — but the cops notice.

Driving: W accelerates, S brakes, A/D steer, Space handbrakes, Shift boosts. Hit pedestrians or police and your wanted level climbs.

Wanted: Stars in the top-right. Police chase you harder with each star. Break line of sight and stay quiet to cool down. Get caught and you're BUSTED.

Missions: Follow the gold marker. Press 1, 2 or 3 on the start screen to jump to a mission briefing. Complete all three to finish the story — then keep the city.

Saving: Progress saves automatically. Use Continue on the start screen.
